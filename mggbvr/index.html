Metal Gear: Ghost Babel VR Missions

Metal Gear: Ghost Babel VR Missions is a part of Metal Gear: Ghost Babel. It features 90 missions, many of which are de-makes of Metal Gear Solid: VR Missions. The 90 missions have a practice and time attack mode. All speedruns are done on the time attack mode.
