Air Walking Glitch

First found and reported by MattMatt:

Facts about this mechanic:

  • This trick works on PC and Console alike
  • it does not work on 1.0.0 on ps5 from the disc install without oatches
  • it does work since 1.1.1 (tested on Xbox on that Version)
  • it still works in 1.1.2 (originally found on this patch)
  • this trick works with walking, crouch walking, crawling
  • air walking at 30fps is more effective than 60fps
  • swapping between 2 camos is at least 2x more effective than spamming the same camo over and over again (while it still works)
  • air walking over stairs (going up) and slopes (that'd you'd slide down) gives a massive height boost on top of the forward momentum as shown by Metsa https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BUzSSb51WkA
  • you can NOT skip past codecs and cutscene triggers, however codec triggers seemingly given a random heigh boost
  • if skipping the ocelot unit, you will not get the missed items (mk.22, m1911 and scientist uniform) into your inventory after the waterfall/torture segment or before the bike chase (where a missing rpg for example would be added for you if you had not picked it up before)

It allows to skip the following bosses:

  • Ocelot Unit
  • The Pain
  • The Fury Fight trigger (but not the fight itself) as shown by MarlonH8 https://youtu.be/qOHfAdt9x5U

General Info

Set the game framerate to 30, as lower FPS allows you to move further during this glitch.

Ocelot Unit Skip

If standing on top of the roof, with the right angle, you can take 20 camo swaps and you'll be far enough over to be behind the gate. From there just walk into the load zone trigger and move on as usual.

Note: You won't have the m1911, the mk.22 and the scientist uniform because you effectively skip the eva cutscene that triggers receiving these items.

Lab + Fear Skip

We can use the camo swap at the first set of stairs (or other sets) in the warehouse during our first visit to go behind the locked door.

Full explanation by Jaguar King:

  • https://www.twitch.tv/jaguar_king/clip/FlirtySavoryBaguetteMoreCowbell-qgaUi5ZoK02BbAei

Note that Metsa is swapping between two guns to get a boost on the stairs to save swap animations and not needing too long to get over the wall.

While the first successful lab skip was done by MarlonH8, Jaguar King was the first person to slowly unlock the method to reliably make this subarea load properly so we can leave the level:
https://www.twitch.tv/jaguar_king/clip/FlirtySavoryBaguetteMoreCowbell-qgaUi5ZoK02BbAei

One full execution from entering Warehouse 1 to doing both tricks to leaving the level by Hau5test:

Notes about the sequence:

  • You can do this skip on PC and console, there's no version difference!
  • For the stairs going up you want to swap between two guns, this will give you a small boost,
  • off that small boost you want to begin your camo swaps, around 30 times is enough to skip from the first set of stairs over to the other side of the wall
  • once you're in the other room, you need to cook a grenade (any grenade type works) or any other throwable item (like a fish)
  • while walking into the corner for 2-ish seconds, once done, quickly lean against the door,
  • then you can go up the stairs and the load zone should be active so you can leave the area and effectively skip the lab and the fear boss fight.

Air + Sky Walking Glitch

On September 28th 2025 MetalGlennSolid shared this curious clip with the community:

It shows him using the camo swap to Airwalk Glitch to then skip the Ocelot Unit fight, but instead of falling down after having stopped the camo swapping, he stayed in the air, similar to sky-walking. Seemingly the sleeping sniper body allowed Snake to perform the sky-walking glitch and stay in the air. This was then attempted to be recreated by SuaveBeardson with some degree of sucess however it seemingly still was very low on the successrate itself.

At this point in time, there was no reliable way to recreate this setup that allows fewer camo swaps and getting this sky-walking state. It would mean another significant time-save over doing the boss fight itself, but alas - further testing required.

Sleepwalking / Dead Man walking Bug

Utilizing the Spatsa and while hanging, we can achieve that Snake is asleep and hanging at the same time.
This state causes the game to be permanently confused and allows for interesting follow up situations.
Such as guards being unable to alert on Snake. Snake being able to walk past load triggers into other areas and more.
