Snake Vs. Monkey

"Gotcha. Commencing Operation Ape Snake."

Note: Snake Vs. Monkey is only available on the PS2 versions of Metal Gear Solid 3. The extra stages Return of the Living Apes and The Apes of Wrath are only available on Subsistence. You must complete the game once to unlock all available stages.

Escape from the Jungle

  • Go downleft, down to line up with monkey on cliff.
  • Be careful on your position when throwing first stun, the monkey can go right.
  • The roll AnonymityHope does over the log is very difficult; you can opt to climb over instead.
  • Sonar isn't required, but is helpful.
