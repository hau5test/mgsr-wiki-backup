Metal Gear Solid: VR Missions

Metal Gear Solid: VR Missions released in 1999 to Playstation, and was later ported to PC and PlayStation Network. It is known as Metal Gear Solid: Special Missions in PAL regions. Focusing on the gameplay of the Metal Gear Solid engine, you play as Snake in over 100+ VR missions1). From weapons and sneaking to puzzles and score attacks, there is much to do in this game. In Japan, VR Missions was bundled with the Integral release of Metal Gear Solid. Outside of Japan, consumers had to buy VR1 separately.
