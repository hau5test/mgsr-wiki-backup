How to skip Johnny soiling his pants

As Rat Patrol reaches the end of the stairs, punch Johnny once.
That will trigger Johnny having his typical bowel irritations.
Once you hear him make his bowel movement noises, punch him again.
The 2nd punch will then skip the segment and you can move on into the area.

Continue towards the toilets and wait at the outside corner for Rat Patrol to come in.
If Johnny is cowering in fear, you can shoot him once to get him out of his animation.
Go where the wall will collapse and open up. Don't stand too close or it will knock you down.

Once the wall is open, enter the room and use the Big Boss mask to knock out all the frogs in the opened up area.

It's time to use three of your SG Satchels one after another

Wait still in the area towards the center of the building and shoot the incoming frogs.

Once all the frogs are gone, you can move on to the laser area.

Let Meryl wake Johnny up and move on.

Bottom Floor

After the cutscene played, you can drop down the hole and enter the final area.

The bulldozer will crash into the building and 2 stun grenades will be used by the enemy. Turn around to not get hit by them.

As the stuns explode - it's time to use your 4th SG Satchel that you placed above.

Rat Patrol will kill 2 of the frogs and the Big Boss mask will take care of the rest usually.

It's done - time to move down the elevator and go to the next area.

Frogs Encounter

https://youtu.be/yIA28CWrw3s?t=2247
There's only going to be 4 frogs coming in for Liquid Easy difficulty.

As the fight starts turn backwards and face right. Get your MK2 equipped and lie down on the floor.
You will shoot down the hallway where the screens are and hit 2 frogs in the legs to knock them out.

Turn back around to the left at the nearby door way with the glass windows. Get the Operator out.
2 more frogs will jump in, they'll be turned away from you. Shoot the ground so they face you.
The Big Boss mask will take care of them instantly. Time to move on to the main fight of this Act.
