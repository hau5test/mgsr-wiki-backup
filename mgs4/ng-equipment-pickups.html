Your Complete New Game Arsenal

Return to Metal Gear Solid 4

In New Game your aim is to collect and buy as few unique items and weapons as possible in order to simplify menuing and reduce time loss from menu & memo screens, the minimal amount of must collect items are listed below.
Items below may have additional locations they can be found in, but only the most optimal ones for the speedrun are noted.
Some items are only better or worth picking up in a Rank 1 (Big Boss etc.) run and are donated as such.
Note: for the purpose of Any% runs, it is assumed the player will be using the code 14893 during act 4, thus requiring only 60,000 DP (100,000 if not on sale). Also, some Rank 1 and Optional items may be required on BBH/TBE for points.
