Optimal Liquid Strats

phase 1

  • Dash forward
  • 5 combo
  • if L.block strong punch
  • L.combo
  • loop

phase 2

  • Dash forward
  • strong punch
  • dash forward
  • loop till L.dodge
  • 5 combo or S.dodge windup and 5 combo
  • repeat from loop
  • 1 punch loop watch elbow

phase 3

  • Dash forward
  • strong punch
  • dash backwards
  • let liquid dash to you
  • loop if lucky
  • if L.block bait and dodge/interrupt L.combo
  • loop
  • if open hand try to force L.dash or strong punch/body blow to force swap at cost of cutscenes: timing matters
  • in general, let liquid dodge to you for easy loop

phase 4

the last 3 hits are free

the game literally forces you to take them and doesnt let liquid attack
