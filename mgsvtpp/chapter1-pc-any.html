MGSV:TPP Tutorials and Guides

Episode 1 Phantom Limbs

Example by MetsaMetsa

Tutorial by MetalGlennSolid

Part 1 - Tutorial

The tutorial section involves a sequence of small tasks, so do as follows:

  • Open the iDroid, close it after Ocellot says -“…first priority.”
  • Open the Int-scope and aim at the village ASAP.
  • Press the radio button after Ocellot says -“I’ll tell you anything I can about it.”
  • Place a marker on the village and close the iDroid.

Part 2 - Rescue Miller

Ride D-Horse to Da Ghwandai Khar. In the town ride D-Horse all the way to the main building, where Miller is. Get off D-Horse, shoot the 1 guard next to the building and 1 through the window, inside. Get through the window, rescue Miller and get on D-Horse. Move towards the LZ, buffering the Phantom Cigar on the way. Get off of D-horse after you hear a sound cue. Get back on D-Horse and trigger the chopper cutscene. After the cutscene, turn around horse and shoot at the skulls. Ride to the actual LZ, you can ignore skulls completely. Get off of D-Horse as close to the chopper as you can, while keeping it far enough so Pequod can land. Get Miller into the chopper with you, obvously.

Episode 2 Diamond Dogs

Example by WizardHwK - Note: Shooting the guard at the start is not mandatory and is discouraged in full game runs, as it can cost you a lot of time if you fail to execute it. Although this is a IL the same route can be used in full runs.

Tutorial by MetalGlennSolid

Start quick diving towards Ocelot ASAP (you're forced to walk in this section). Skip the cutscene and fulton 3 guards. Open your iDroid and staff management tab. Wait for Ocellot's cue and mash back, then select the R&D tab, mashing select (K+M left click & spacebar) to develop the C.Box and drop it in. You can perform a dialog skip by shooting Ocellot before you get in to box, it can be a bit finicky to perform it at first. Wait until you can call in the chopper and leave.

Episode 4 C2W

Example by MetsaMetsa - Note: The use of the Phantom Cigar at the start is not required and it's slower than the normal strat.

Tutorial by MetalGlennSolid

Q: “Why C2W?” A: It's faster than Over the Fence.

Chopper skip [Prologue + Up + Chain Up] Deploy at 1800

Develop a Sniper rifle, get on D-horse and ride to the Eastern Communications Post. Buffer the Phantom Cigar on the way. At the comms post, throw a grenade inside the building with the central communications unit. Exit hot zone, you'll know when you're out when you hear a sound cue, and get off D-horse as the alert ends to fast forward to the score screen.

After, or in, C2W develop:

  • RENOV-ICKX SR (Grade 1)
  • C-4 (Grade 1)

From Free Roam, Return to ACC.

Episode 6 Where Do the Bees Sleep

Example by MetsaMetsa

Tutorial by MetalGlennSolid

Chopper skip [Prologue + Up] Start ASAP

Ride all the way to Da Smasei Laman. Go through the caves to the Honey Bee, shoot guards on the way if you deem necessary. After you get the Honey Bee, ride out of the caves with D-Horse, equipping the NV-Goggles and the Honey Bee on the way out. Skip the cutscenes and, when you gain control of Snake, turn around and aim at the stairs. Keep shooting until all Skulls are dead. Destroy the anti air radar and, after Miller says he'll call in Pequod, open your iDroid and select the closes LZ. Buffer the Phantom Cigar and stop it after Pequod arives. Develop C4.

Quiet Skip Section - Episode 8 or 10?

Episode 8 strat is roughly 40~45 seconds faster by itself compared to Episode 10, and it potentially saves time on medical platform. You can still do Episode 10 instead of 8 if you want to play it safe as Episode 8 is really risky.


Episode 8 Occupation Forces (Faster)

Example by MetsaMetsa

Use C4 to blowup the first tank, get in the chopper and use the minigun to take out the remaining targets. Shoot at the target in passenger seat of the truck and take out last tank.

Episode 9 Backup Back Down

Example by MetsaMetsa

Tutorial by MetalGlennSolid

Land on northern LZ

Place three C4 charges on the road, use the Phantom Cigar and blow up the C4 after the APC cancel your cigar. Get on the side of the road closer to LZ, cigar again and stop it when you see 1:00 on the timer. Timer should end right as cigar ends. There is a prisoner transport driving towards you with guaranteed A+ R&D prisoner. Shoot 2 wheels with RENOV and fulton the prisoner. Leave towards north.

Free Roam Section

Example by MetsaMetsa

Tutorial by MetalGlennSolid

Ride towards Serak Power Plant. Pick up 100 Common metals from a house on the way. Extract the guards on the outpost before Serak Power Plant and get 200 common metals and 200 fuel resources. After materials are extracted do following menus:

  • R&D and Support Units to level 6
  • Develop Chopper level 2
  • R&D Unit to level 7
  • Develop the Smoke Grenade, FAKEL and Grade 2 Tranq. Pistol.
  • Base Development Unit to 9, if possible, to make the medical platform timer shorter.

Go to Serak power plant and get the invoice and return to ACC. In the ACC you can upgrade the chopper to grade 2, it saves a bit of menuing later on.

Episode 3 - Fast-travel

Example by MetsaMetsa

Tutorial by MetalGlennSolid

Chopper skip [Episode 2 + Down] Remember to bring the smoke grenades.

Run towards the Delivery point, no need to checkpoint as you should have time to spare. Grab 200 common metals inside the building and get the invoice on the delivery point. Get in a box an keep checking out the medical platform timer. Try to see when the minutes change so you know when medical platform will be done. Fast travel to Serak Power Plant when it's done. Episode 12 Hellbound

Example by MetsaMetsa

Tutorial by MetalGlennSolid

Tranquilize the guard outside the gate. Throw a smoke in to the jeep and drive. The smoke won’t hide you from the gunship, but it prevents you from taking too much damage from the soldiers. Drive along the road taking the path shown in the vod. Before the container with the 3 guards, use the smoke trick again. Use another smoke near the base camp and drive to Emmerich’s “lab”.

After the cutscene reset from checkpoint, call the chopper to nearby LZ and use a phantom cigar before Miller calls you on radio. Get Emmerich on the jeep, throw another smoke and drive to LZ (This smoke is optional). Skip cutscene and put Emmerich back into the jeep, throw another smoke in and drive through Sahelanthropus to the LZ. Wait for the chopper to land and get in. You can save some time here by automatically ordering staff. Use the minigun to shoot down the drones Sally releases, and shoot at Sally (Bonus swag if you “tranquilize” Sahelanthropus).

Fast Mother Base Exit to Episode 2

Episode 13 Pitch Dark

Example by MetsaMetsa with Pitch Dark Warp and Soldier Pickup Warp

Tutorial by MetalGlennSolid

Equip the FAKEL and Deploy ASAP

Important Menuing:

  • Level up the Support Unit to level 9.
  • Place a marker for exiting mission (A in the image on the right)

Call in D-Horse and ride through the forest area. Ride through the village using the FAKEL to take out guards in your way. After you pass trough the village call in a bombardment on the oil separator tank and Perform the Pitch Dark Warp, if possible. After black screen, either, call for D-Horse and head to the start of episode 14 or, perform the Soldier Pickup Warp.

If you fail to perform Pitch Dark Warp you can wait for the bombardment to destoy the oil separator tank, that will warp you near the oilfield. You'll need to infiltrate the oilfield and shutdown the transfer pump. After that you can try and perform the Soldier Pickup Warp or ride to the start of episode 14.

On Free Roam, Select Episode 14 and ride D-Horse to the starting point or, if you've exited the mission by Soldier Pickup Warp, return to the ACC.

Episode 18 Blood Runs Deep

Example by MetsaMetsa (With Soldier Pickup Warp)

Tutorial by MetalGlennSolid (No Soldier Pickup Warp/ Safer strat)


Part 1 - Sniping and Wandering

Call for pickup on the LZ at Kungenga Mine. Snipe the 1st target with the FAKEL, or sniper, from the start. Ride D-walker towards the mine, don't fear the tank seeing you on the way. Before the mine you can call in an airstrike on the anti air radar (setup for riskier strat). Ride into the mine all the way to the backdoor (refer to VOD for path). Destroy the guard tower and open the door with the FAKEL. Call the chopper to the nearby LZ (if you've destroyed the anti air radar) and run to the kids.


Part 2 - DO NOT LET THOSE KIDS DIE

After the cutscene, get the disable kid and tranquilize at least one of them:

  • Run to the front door, try not to get spotted. Tranquiliza a guard near some wood planks. Get the disabled kid on chopper. Perform a Pickup Warp with the soldier you've tranquilized near the wood planks (risky strat with Pickup Warp strat).
  • Run to the front door, avoid getting spotted like the plague. Get on the chopper, use the minigun to kill as many soldiers as possible and destroy the enemy gunship (risky strat without Soldier Pickup).
  • Run to the back door. Get on D-Walker and run to the LZ with the kid. Burst to the front gates of the mine in a slight angel, this should get you through. Get on the chopper and destroy the enemy gunship, if needed (safer strat).

Episode 20 Voices

Example by MetsaMetsa

Tutorial by MetalGlennSolid

Chopper skip [Prologue + Up] Deploy at 1800


Part 1 - The Ride

Manipulate D-Walker's spawn with the camera. Ride D-Walker to the valley. Get off D-Walker, dive under the log and run to the tunnel. Call D-walker halfway through the tunnel, it should spawn on the other side. Shoot the gate open by blowing up the barrel next to it with sniper, or with the FAKEL. Ride D-Walker and leave him next to the door to the Devil's House. Run in and use the FAKEL, or sniper, to kill the first body, removing the cutscene trigger. Run to Shabani and, after the black screen, skip the cutscene.


Part 2 - Fight Fire With Water

Run outside and get onto D-walker. Ride to the pool, positioning yourself near the end of a tree trunk that's in the pool, and manipulate the Man on Fire’s teleport position, so he spawns next to the pool. After a moment fire pillar will appear. The Third Child will appear too, revealing where the Man on Fire will spawn. Move into position so when you run over him with D-walker he falls into the water. If you hear him screen, that means you're good. Ride out through the tunnel.

Leave Mother Base by chopper and Level up R&D Unit to level 13. Develop the GROM rocket launcher, Gatling Gun for D-Walker and level up the Support Unit to at least level 9. Equip the Gatling Gun before at least before Episode 23 (White Mamba).

Episode 28 Code Talker

Example by MetsaMetsa (With D-Horse Clip)

Tutorial by MetalGlennSolid (D-Walker strat)

Chopper skip [Prologue + Up] Deploy at 1800/ ASAP with D-Horse (faster) or D-Walker (slower)


Part 1 - Skulls

D-Walker - There’s a few ways you can deal with the skull effectively, you can either just drive through the area and hit the skulls with D-Walker's Gatling Gun, or you can use the smoke trick on D-Walker and go straight to the mansion.

D-Horse - The fastests way. Ride D-Horse with the Fakel in hand and shoot the closes Skull as soon as it appears. Ride to the mansion avoiding the Skulls. If the Skulls spot you, try zig-zagging to avoid getting shot.


Part 2 - Mansion

D-Walker - Tranquilize shot to distract the searchlight guard, ride up the hill, don’t get spotted, and get over the fence towards the door. Without chicken hat throw a smoke next to the door, dive, and after smoke has spread you can run in. With chicken hat, just fakel the guys outside and run in.

D-Horse Clip - It’s possible to clip into Code Talker’s room with the D-Horse.


Part 3 - Escape

Checkpoint after Code Talkers cutscene to skip the walking section. Call in chopper to the nearby LZ and get there. With good movement you’ll be there before the chopper.

Episode 31 Sahelanthropus

Example by MetsaMetsa

Tutorial by MetalGlennSolid

Run to D-walker and ride to a APC. Get on the APC and switch to the rockets. Drive the APC towards Sally while shooting the rockets then start reversing a little. You want to avoid the flamethrower and get the sword attack instead. Shoot at his open chest with the automatic cannon during the sword attack. When Sally is closing its chest, switch to missiles and shoot all of them at him. If everything goes perfectly it should put Sally into the “roaring” phase where the chest is open again, shoot at it with the automatic cannon. After a while shoot another salvo of missiles. After you’ve reached 50% of its health, if you’ve got a opportunity, shoot the white “tubes” on its back. Otherwise just prioritize Sally's chest to interrupt its movements and keep shooting the missiles. If Sally is still alive after your tank is destroyed, use D-Walker's Gatling Gun on the white “tubes” on Sally's back to finish it off. If you messed up bad, your APC is gone and Sally has too much HP, it can be faster to just reset.
