Episode 19: On the Trail

Any% w/ Chopper skip

  • Chopper skip into the mission. Time of day shouldn't matter. D-walker as buddy Support unit at level 9 or higher.
  • Head towards Outpost #15 and make sure to get spotted.
    • This makes our target to move to A marker.
  • Get off D-walker at roughly 330 meters away from the marker. The exact distance is more so need to experiment how far you can be.
  • Call bombardment and use phantom cigar.
    • You can start the cigar first and do the menuing quickly but if you mess it up, the target escapes and you get a game over.
  • Airstrike should hit the target during phantom cigar, going straight to scoreboard skipping the end dialogue.

Example

Any% Chopper skipless

  • Select Eastern LZ and 1800 for deployment time, D-walker as buddy. Support unit at level 9 or higher.
  • During chopper ride into the mission open map and place waypoint on the target
    • Red lines shown in the image help with lining up the shot. Southern edge of the road and shape of the hill south of the target.
  • Before you hop off the chopper buffer a phantom cigar, and use the radio to carry it over on D-walker.
  • Ride towards the marker
  • Call airstrike when you are 580 meters or close from the marker
  • Get off D-walker at 275-280 meters.
  • Airstrike should hit the target during phantom cigar, going straight to scoreboard skipping the end dialogue.
