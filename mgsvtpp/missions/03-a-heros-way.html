Guard Pathing Manipulation

You can manipulate the commander's pathing by doing as follows:

  • Go into free roam at day time and head to “Da Shago Kallai” (Shago Village), turn off the generator and leave the area without being spotted.
  • Go to the mission starting spot and use the phantom cigar until night, then start the mission.
  • Head back to “Da Shago Kallai” and the commander should be walking towards you.
    • You can eliminate him however you see fit. But, this specific strategy is more useful in No Traces/ PSNK runs.
