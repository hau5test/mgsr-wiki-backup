Metal Gear Solid V: The Phantom Pain (TPP)

"V has come to."

Metal Gear Solid V: The Phantom Pain released in September 2015 for PC, PS3, PS4, Xbox360 and Xbox One. It is the final Metal Gear game Hideo Kojima directed. Compared to Ground Zeroes' small island playground, The Phantom Pain features two open world areas - Afghanistan and Central Africa. It expands Peace Walker's base management, equipment development, and Fulton, allowing for a great deal of customization and approaches. Its troubled development and release left many fans ambivalent.
