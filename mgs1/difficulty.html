Very Easy

  • Addition of MP5, Infinite Ammo, Silenced, Fully Automatic Gun. Replaces FAMAS.
  • Initial load is slower on console
  • RAM Manipulation is impossible
  • No Score Screen
  • Assume Easy otherwise
