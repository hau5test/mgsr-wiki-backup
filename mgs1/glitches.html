Out Of Bounds Kick Back

Recreation

  • Crawl backwards against a special step.
  • Stand up (while also leaning into the step other snake will refuse to stand up)
  • Go prone.
  • On step 3 snake does a kick back into the steps. This kickback was removed in Intergral so he just lays forward.

So far it only works on Nuke Bldg 1, Tower A/B roof, and Blast steps.

!! To actually go OOB in Nuke and Blast, the stand up animation has to be “cut in half” !!

  • Snake needs to stand up but before being fully upright, go prone.
  • The PSG1 makes this simple. Box is needed for Nuke bldg because Campbell said so

Permanent Infinite Grenades via Headless Scope Glitch

Headless Scope Glitch

Originally found in 2018 by Tromboncino, we can achieve a headless state
while using the scope in the PC 2000 version of MGS Integral

The basic idea is as follows:

  • have no weapon in hand
  • equip the scope
  • use a weapon hotkey to add a weapon into your hand
  • double press triangle to enter fpv mode
  • because the scope already is a form of fpv, instead of going into fpv mode you're now in regular camera, while the scope is active
  • from here you can keep using your grenades even when they're empty, but they only explode in your hand

Permanent Infinite Grenades (Console)

You'd think, because we need the headless scope glitch, that this result of getting infinite grenades is only possible on the PC 2000 release.
But as it turns out, that is not true! BigBossSnake has tinkered more with the game on emulator and found out on August 18th that we can indeed
get a similar result to that of headless scope glitch on console. Here's his finding in video form:

The method is as follows:

  • lower your weapon of choice ammo count to 0 (chaffs in this video example)
  • while the grenade goes out, hold R2 quickly and switch off to another weapon
  • this way the 0/X amount of ammo stays in your inventory
  • now perform a weapons glitch
  • in this video example it was done with the grenade
  • switch to the chaffs with 0 ammo
  • cook a grenade in your hand to get -1 ammo (glitched state)
  • cook a 2nd grenade in your hand not with the glitched state and let it explode
  • setup is complete
  • now go through torture, let your inventory be taken away
  • on retrieval of your inventory, you have now your glitched permanent infinite grenades (chaffs in this example)

Hind-D skip level up sequence for an IGT time save (banned under current ruleset)
found by iLL_Pazzo applicable for all difficulties:

  • Take the Stinger ammo box before the Hind ladder
  • Take the ladder and start the Hind fight
  • Kill the Hind WITHOUT (very important) using GME
  • When the Hind reached 0 health, just suicide
  • Take the ladder again and leave the roof
  • Go down the stairs and go to Wolf2 using GME2
  • Congratulation, you just saved 25 to 30 seconds (depending on how fast you suicide) on every category without even using GME or area reload 🙂
