Choke Skip

  • It was first shown by K'Will in 2013. According to him, he found it back in 2004. Original video : https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tNOl-uB-zV8
  • It only works on Extreme and European Extreme.
  • Execution : First start running towards the southeast Tengu with a katana. Punch him and start choking him, start counting from 1.
  • Move to the room, southwest spot. Whenever he starts moving his arms, choke him again and add +1 to the count.
  • Count until 10 chokes then release him. You must not choke him 11 times because he will die.
  • - Basic version
  • Re-grab him. Whenever he starts moving his arms, release him and re-grab him. Do this until he passes out.
  • - Faster version : https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z_Mm_K7-DH0
  • Re-grab him. Choke 3 times whenever he is moving his arms, without releasing him. After this, choke 3 times with release at each choke. This is called “10-3-3*” (10 fast, 3 fast, 3 slow).

Coolant Skip

  • Useful to skip Tengu 2 on all difficulties for Sons of Liberty
  • Possible on later versions, but not consistent
  • Equip M9 or PSG1T, tranquilize Snake
  • Run to the edge of the circle on the bottom
  • Equip coolant spray, spray Tengus left to right, right to left.
  • Release square after each pass to not run out of spray.
  • This trick saves 20 to 30 seconds
