Metal Gear Solid 2: VR Missions

Metal Gear Solid 2: VR Missions released in 2002 in the Substance release to Playstation 2, and was later ported to Xbox, PC, Xbox360, Playstation 3, Playstation Vita, and Nvidia Shield. Focusing on the gameplay of the Metal Gear Solid 2 engine, you play as various incarnations of Snake and Raiden.

  • To unlock all missions without playing through them, name your savefile “UUDDLRLRBA” aka the Konami Code
