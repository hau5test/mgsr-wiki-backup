Crawlspace Upclip

Used For:

  • Snake (MGS1) Bomb Disposal 01
  • maybe more

Tutorial Video:

  • A universal technique to clip onto any crawlspace, often resulting in Out of Bounds Glitches. Found by MarlonH8, this glitch finds its main use in the main game's Prez Parkour skip, but can also be used at the same location for OOB routes of Bomb Disposal 01 speedruns and possibly in other stages. Can save multiple seconds.

Damage Cancel

  • By holding L2 or R2 at the exact set of frames before receiving damage, you can negate all damage received. Very precise technique.

Dav Drop

Used For:

  • Snake (MGS1) Sneaking 09
  • Snake Variety 01

Tutorial Video:

  • A Dav Drop or davWILDSTAR is a fall animation cancelling glitch found by an older VR2 runner called terminatortbm (also known as davWILDSTARtbm). It allows the player to cancel the animation of Raiden/Snake falling down a ledge by having no weapons equipped and pressing square on the correct frame. Saves approximately three-quarters of a second.

Dav Drop (FPV)

Used For:

  • First Person View Mode

Tutorial Video:

  • A FPV Mode-only variant of the Dav Drop found by MarlonH8. Push and hold your Left and Right Sticks in opposite, horizontal directions, then slightly move the Left Stick up. You are free to have a weapon equipped while performing the FPV variant. Saves approximately three-quarters of a second.

Holding Up Guards

Used For:

  • Snake (MGS1) Hold Up 1-10

Tutorial Video:

  • A basic mechanic of the game is to hold a guard with a weapon. Stand relatively close enough to them (without the guards spotting you) and hold down square to lift your weapon. This can also be done by equipping weapons which instantly take you into FPV such as the PSG1, PSG1-T, and Stinger.
  • Doing this with weapons such as the AK-74u or M4 would mean you would eventually shoot the guard. The game wants you to lightly hold the square on a controller with pressure sensitive controls such as the DualShock 2 or DualShock 3. On platforms that don't support this, it might be possible to hold down L3 while holding the Square button to replicate this game mechanic. On PC, this is one option, another option is to go into the V's Fix settings and change your controller type to “V's Type.” This basically replaces the L3 mechanic mentioned earlier for R1.

Quick KO

Used For:

  • Eliminate All
  • Elimination

Tutorial Video:

  • A useful way to quickly to eliminate guards in a non-lethal way. Saves a bullet and the guards fall onto the floor really fast, allowing for the bodies to disappear faster than a tranq-headshot.
  • This can be done with almost any weapon in the game such as: M9, USP, Socom, Grenades, Stun Grenades, Chaff Grenades, Magazines, Claymores, C4, M4, AK-74u, and the RGB6.

Quick Stand-Up

Used For:

  • Raiden Variety 01
  • Snake Variety 01

Tutorial Video:

  • Usually, when crawling out of a vent, the game will force Raiden/Snake to move entirely out of it before allowing the player to rise. Using this technique, the player can skip that and instantly rise up.
  • Right before exiting the vent, press R1 to go into FPV to allow this glitch to work.
  • Found by MarlonH8.

Silent Shot

Used For:

  • Various Levels

Tutorial Video:

  • A useful strat to get through tough guard cycles.
  • To do this, pause the game about the same time you try to do any action that would cause a guard(s) to move away from the designated patrol routes. (i.e: yellow/red cones on Radar)
  • Difficult skill, almost frame-perfect.
  • Pausing means: Pressing Start, opening the Item/Weapon menu, or using the Codec.
