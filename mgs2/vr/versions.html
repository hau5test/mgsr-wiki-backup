Versions

MGS2 VR Missions is only available on the Substance and HD versions of the game. It is NOT available on Sons of Liberty.

Note: Certain versions/platforms/regions can affect the optimal strats for specific levels.

Substance

Platforms:

  • PlayStation 2
  • Xbox
  • PC

Note: The Document of MGS2 has 5 VR Missions and is unique to the PlayStation 2

HD Edition

Platforms:

  • PlayStation 3
  • PSNow
  • PSTV
  • PSVita
  • Xbox 360
  • XboxOne
  • Nvidia Shield

Regions

Regions:

  • NTSC-U (60Hz only)
  • NTSC-J (60Hz only)
  • PAL (50 or 60Hz depending platform)

The differences between regions include different voice acting and the game playing at a different frame rate.
