Metal Gear Solid 2: VR Missions has a total of 511 individual levels across 7 different characters:

  • Raiden
  • Raiden (Ninja)
  • X Raiden
  • Snake
  • Pliskin
  • Snake (Tuxedo)
  • Snake (MGS1)

The Document of Metal Gear Solid 2 also has a total of 5 VR levels:

  • The Document of Metal Gear Solid 2
