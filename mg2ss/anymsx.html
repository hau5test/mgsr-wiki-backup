Metal Gear 2 Any% (MSX) Tutorial

While there won't be a big video tutorial currently, the run that was performed by Hau5test can pose as a blueprint for the time being.
Most of the aspects are similar to the PS2 version's original difficulty. Things that are different will be noted on this page.

Meet Black Ninja

Infiltration Point

Right away you'll notice that Snake's movement is slower compared to the PS2/HDC counterparts. But guards also have worse vision in some aspects.
First thing you should do is go to the north-east truck to get the B1 ration. It'll be useful in the Black Ninja fight.

As you walk up to the north-east fence to infiltrate, you can comfortably wait after the fence before turning left to punch the guard or let him pass without alert.

Follow the path as usual. When you get past the fences into the opening area of Zansibarland - punch the boxes (tanks?) once to distract the 3 guards.
Keep walking right to get to the trucks where the pistol is contained. Punch or kill the right guard and then make your way up into the vent to enter the facility.

Zanzibar Bldg. 1F

You can bypass all guards without alert by first knocking on the wall to distract the upper level guard.
Walk left to get on this elevated position and walk past him without touching the guard.

Keep walking and stay to the upper wall when turning left towards the elevators. See on the radar when the guard turns left to get on your screen.
Walk into the screen with the elevators just before the guard walks off screen. Timing isn't crucial but it helps to not have an alert and keep maximum health.

Zanzibar Bldg. 2F

Leave the elevator and turn right. We need the level 1 card out of the room from the East.
It is worth waiting for the guard to enter the screen and have him hear you when making noises on the floor.
After the guard hears the footsteps, enter the right housing and get the level 1 card.
The distracted guard outside will turn around and not see you, as you walk back to the elevator.
Take the elevator up to 3F.

Black Ninja (Black Color)

Beating the Ninja on the MSX version seemingly does have no manipulation like it does on PS2 and Xbox (HDC).
Try your best to stay at the bottom side of the boxes and anticipate Ninja's standing spots. He'll always throw 3 stars
before moving again. Use the ration from the beginning to be able to take some hits and retaliate for a quicker boss fight.

Post Black Ninja

Leave north and enter the top right room to get the gas mask. it'll make the running man fight much easier.
To leave north, equip the level 2 card. To enter the gas mask room you need the level 1 card.

After having grabbed the gas mask, save and load the game to warp back to the elevator. Take it down to 1F.

Running Man

Running man takes 7 mines to kill. You can't place the mines on top of each other like in the PS2 version.
So place one in the usual spot, then run up and place a mine at the north hallway.
Keep running clockwise and place a mine in the hallway between the top right and bottom right room.
Keep doing circles until all 7 mines have hit running man. If done correctly you will barely survive with enough health.

IMPORTANT: place the mines further away from the screen border or else Running Man won't actually trigger the mines when walking over them

Here's the 3 wall spots:

Tower Bldg. 1F (Spiral)

Walk all the way to the center elevator and take it up to meet Red Blaster.

Red Blaster

Because the spider silk lines take way longer to break and have Snake continue walking,
the general suggestion is to beat Red Blaster in the first room with the elevator door.

One variant can be to start walking right and breaking the two left spider silk lines.
Then constantly juggle Red Blaster going left and right and throwin grenades onto the ceiling.

REMEMBER: you can't stay close to the top wall, because you grenades will land in the out of bounds and just vanish.

Generally keep to the middle wall, or bottom of the upper walkway to have the right distance.
Walk left or right, tap up to turn up, throw grenade, keep moving to either side and loop until the fight is over.

Tower Roof Pigeon

Walk up to the tower roof and explode the wall to enter the rooftop.
Put on the B2 ration to get the pigeon to land and get the secret message.

Call 140.51 to reach Kio Marv
Call 140.82 to reach Petrovich Madnar

Then walk back out again. You can save and load to teleport, but because of the longer load times
and the run (as of now) being timed RTA, it may not be worth it teleporting back outside of the rooftop.

This is where you get teleported to. If the pigeon lands on one of the bottom screens, it is faster to just walk out of the rooftop.

Zanzibar Bldg. 4F

Unlike the PS2/HDC pathing, you can actually just sneak past the camera at the north end path without being seen.
Similar to the PS2/HDC version of the game, you have to wait a short moment before keep on walking before the guard sees you.
That way you can make it through the floor without an alert.

Escape

Ideally you have at least one more ration left over from the Big Boss fight or you can
grab a free ration after leaving the elevator when reaching the forest level.

Both at the elevator and at the final screen you need to kill plenty of guards for up to 2 minutes.
Time ends when Snake says “I'm out of ammo” and the cutscene starts playing.
