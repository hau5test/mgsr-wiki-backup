Twin Snakes Very Easy Route

  • All guards will fall asleep upon being shot with the M9. All guards will be knocked out when you roll or throw them.
  • Hind-D, Wolf 1, Wolf 2, MG-REX take the least damage to kill
  • Pick up the M9 near where you spawn at the Docks
  • There are stun grenades in the lockers at the Docks, use these to perform the Prozac Skip on the Elevator
  • Use the second stun grenade to glitch through the Darpa Chief Door using the Prozac Skip
  • No guard appears on the elevator at the Docks.
  • You don't need a ration until Disc 2 if you play well.
  • You can run into the spotlight, or use a stun grenade to start an alert at the Helipad.
  • You do not need the chaff for Nuke Bldg 1F door skip, so hold up and then tap upright after 3 steps forward at the Canyon.
  • You can equip the M9 in Nuke Bldg 1F, so use that for door skip. Each guard needs one shot to fall asleep.
  • When alerting Meryl to your presence in Nuke Bldg B1, fire the SOCOM in the hallway before entering the office area. Then hold downleft to get into Meryl's sight.
  • You do not need Pentaz to steady your aim, and you can get by with only picking up the ammo loaded in the PSG1-T.
  • For Wolf1, you need a combination of 3 headshots and 1 body shot. This is unique to Very Easy
  • M9 in the Canyon revisit is very good
  • Go to the left from your starting position to fight Raven
  • Refer to Normal for everything else
