Twin Snakes Easy Notes

  • This difficulty is more similar to Very Easy than Normal.
  • Guards do not fall asleep after one M9 shot, but they will fall asleep fairly quickly after 2 shots.
  • Hind-D, Wolf 1, Wolf 2, MG-REX take the less damage to kill
  • Pick up the M9 in the lockers at the Docks.
  • Must deplete M9 ammo to generate a magazine for the Dock Prozac Skip
  • When approaching the Vent guard, fire an M9 shot very early, and fire when he spots you.
  • You do not need the chaff for Nuke Bldg 1F door skip, so hold up and then tap upright after 3 steps forward at the Canyon.
  • You can equip the M9 in Nuke Bldg 1F, so use that for door skip.
  • Fire the M9 in FPV when you get to the second grate by the missiles. Fire the M9 in FPV when you climb the middle of the 2nd staircase.
  • When alerting Meryl to your presence in Nuke Bldg B1, fire the SOCOM in the hallway before entering the office area. Then hold downleft to get into Meryl's sight.
  • Go upleft at the rock at the canyon, take a similar line to Normal.
  • Go to the right from your starting position to fight Raven, so you flank him.
  • Refer to Normal for everything else
